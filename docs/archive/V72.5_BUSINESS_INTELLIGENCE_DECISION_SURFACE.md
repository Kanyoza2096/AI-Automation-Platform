# V72.5 — Business Intelligence / Decision Surface

V72.5 converts V72.4 durable business truth into evidence-backed signals and advisory recommendations for the Business Cockpit.

## Authority boundary
- Recommendations are not decisions.
- `actionAuthorityGranted` is always false.
- No provider, connector, Action Kernel, outbox, policy mutation, approval, or execution occurs here.
- Executable capability resolution only identifies an existing governed capability; it does not invoke it.
- `GOVERNANCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED` is used for uncertain/high-risk cases.
- Missing capabilities are explicit `CAPABILITY_UNAVAILABLE`, never invented.

## Intelligence inputs
Durable V70/V71/V66 truth: objectives, sales opportunities, internal work/risk state, workforce runs, and measured outcomes.

## Recommendation identity
Recommendations receive deterministic SHA-256 fingerprints over canonical evidence-backed content. The migration `102_v72_5_business_intelligence_recommendations.sql` provides durable history storage with a unique workspace/fingerprint constraint. The read surface remains side-effect free; a future worker/materializer can persist recommendations without making GET requests mutating.

## Outcome linkage
Recommendations carry outcome link slots and evidence fingerprints so later outcome attribution can be attached without changing recommendation authority.

## Route separation
Business Cockpit uses `/api/v1/business-command-center/intelligence` through KCPG. The existing System Command Center `/api/v1/command-center/situation` is untouched.
