# V71.15 — Legacy Runtime Fencing & Authority Convergence

## Purpose
V71.15 does not delete Kanyoza's older engines. It makes their authority explicit and prevents legacy consequential execution from becoming a second automation/governance plane.

## Legacy execution fence
The following legacy direct side effects are closed in production:

- `publish_scheduled_content`
- `optimize_content_schedule`
- `queue_brand_revival`
- `restart_failed_service`
- `quarantine_security_issue`
- direct engagement reply/like
- growth invites/cross-engagement
- direct news publication

A fenced request returns `CAPABILITY_UNAVAILABLE`, `authority_granted=false`, and `execution_requested=false`, with the canonical next path recorded.

## Director convergence
Director remains active for observation, intelligence, learning and proposal generation. Its legacy capability execution surface is fenced for consequential capabilities, including after a legacy task has been marked approved. Legacy approval cannot substitute for the canonical governance plane.

## Scheduler/news convergence
The normal content scheduler remains usable through the canonical publish workflow. Legacy direct news publication and direct engagement mutations are fenced because they have no equivalent governed Action Kernel capability yet.

## Canonical execution law
`KCPG -> Business Objective -> V70 -> V71 Workforce -> Governance -> Action Kernel -> durable outbox -> Connector -> Provider -> Reconciliation`

## V69 relationship
V69 remains foundational. Its workflow execution plane orchestrates and reconciles but does not itself perform provider mutations. Its connector runtime gateway remains the governed connector boundary.

## Certification
Offline controls in this package are tested. Live provider execution, production restart/crash persistence, multi-worker races, credential revocation and provider rate-limit behavior remain live evidence requirements and are not claimed as proven here.
