# V72.7 — Business Decision Intelligence & Outcome Attribution

V72.7 connects the durable recommendation ledger to recorded governance decisions and measured business outcomes.

## Boundary
This is an evidence/intelligence surface. It does not create decisions, approve actions, execute actions, call providers, mutate policy, or grant authority.

## Attribution semantics
- **OBSERVED** — a durable recommendation-to-outcome link is recorded.
- **ASSOCIATED** — an outcome is linked through a recorded decision, but causal contribution is not proven.
- **INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE** — no durable chain supports an effectiveness conclusion.

No attribution state is a causal claim.

## Canonical chain
Recommendation → Governance Decision → Governed Execution → Authoritative Result → Business Outcome → Attribution → Learning.

V72.7 observes that chain; it does not replace any component of it.

## Safety invariants
- Tenant scoped.
- Deterministic evidence fingerprints.
- No authority in intelligence records.
- No causal claims from correlation alone.
- Learning cannot rewrite authority.
- Consequential commands continue through KCPG → Governance → Action Kernel → durable outbox → connector/provider → reconciliation.
