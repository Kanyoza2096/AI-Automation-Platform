# V71.4 — Governed AI Workforce: Agent Planning

V71.4 creates an inspectable planning plane. It constructs objectives, constraints,
dependent steps and capability dispositions, but it never executes a step or grants
authority. Every candidate step remains subject to KDO policy, risk, approval,
Action Kernel, durable outbox, connector and reconciliation controls.

## Safety invariants
- planning is not execution
- default planning context is advisory
- dependency cycles are rejected
- capability-unavailable steps are explicit, never invented
- permission checks are re-evaluated at assessment time
- plan and tenant integrity are verified before assessment
- memory can inform planning but cannot become authority
