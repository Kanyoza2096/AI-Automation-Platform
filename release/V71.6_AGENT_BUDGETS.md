# V71.6 — Governed AI Workforce: Cost & Risk Budgets

V71.6 adds bounded resource and risk budgets for AI agents. Budgets provide a
fail-closed admission constraint around observed/requested usage; they do not
provide authority.

## Controls
- tenant-scoped budgets bound to active agent identity
- action-count, cost and risk-unit limits
- fail-closed checks before usage is recorded
- automatic exhaustion when a configured limit is reached
- suspension/expiration states
- negative/impossible usage rejection
- deterministic tamper-detectable fingerprints
- advisory summaries with `authority_granted=false`
- no provider, connector, Action Kernel, approval or autonomy-envelope calls

## Architectural invariant
A budget may answer **"is there enough bounded capacity for this attempt?"**
It may never answer **"is the agent authorized to do this?"**. Governance and
execution controls remain authoritative.
