# V68.12 — Business Event Delivery Fencing

## Purpose
Harden the durable Business Automation event inbox against stale-worker acknowledgements after lease expiry.

## Problem found
The durable delivery completion RPC previously accepted a delivery id and consumer group without proving that the completing worker still owned the delivery lease. A slow worker could therefore finish after its lease was reclaimed by another worker and acknowledge the same delivery, creating a stale-worker race.

## Repair
- Durable claim RPC now returns an explicit claim token.
- Completion requires the exact claim token currently stored on the delivery.
- Missing claim identity fails closed in the bridge.
- A completion that no longer owns the delivery is treated as a control-plane ownership loss rather than silently accepted.
- Existing retry/reclaim semantics remain intact.

## Safety properties
- A stale worker cannot finalize a delivery claimed by a successor worker.
- The event itself remains durably identified by event_id.
- The bridge does not gain provider or side-effect authority.
- No parallel execution path is introduced.
