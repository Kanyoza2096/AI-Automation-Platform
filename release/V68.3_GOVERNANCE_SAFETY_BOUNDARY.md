# V68.3 — Governance Safety Boundary Certification

## Purpose

V68.3 hardens the Council/Decision governance plane so high-risk and critical
recommendations can never become autonomous approvals through routing,
consensus, fallback behavior, stale cache state, or alternate Council entry
points.

## Finding

A real risk-monotonicity defect was found in `DecisionRouter`: a caller-supplied
HIGH/CRITICAL risk classification could be overwritten by a lower action-class
classification when the independent risk engine did not raise it. That made the
caller risk floor non-monotonic and created a future downgrade path.

## Changes

- Added `council/policies/governance_safety.py`.
- Enforced a final, deterministic safety boundary in both `CouncilOrchestrator`
  and `DemocracyEngine`.
- High/critical results cannot remain `APPROVED` or
  `APPROVED_WITH_WARNINGS`; they are escalated for human review.
- Cached results are rechecked at read time.
- Tier-0 auto-approval now requires an actually `TRIVIAL` proposal risk.
- `DecisionRouter` now computes risk monotonically as the maximum of:
  - caller-supplied risk;
  - action-classified risk;
  - independent risk assessment.

## Invariants

1. Risk may only move upward at the routing boundary.
2. High/critical decisions cannot become autonomous approval.
3. Council availability failure cannot turn high/critical actions into approval.
4. Cached governance results cannot bypass the final safety boundary.
5. This module grants no execution authority and performs no side effects.
