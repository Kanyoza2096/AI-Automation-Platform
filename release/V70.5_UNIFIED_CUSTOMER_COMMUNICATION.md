# V70.5 — Unified Customer Communication Operating Loop

## Purpose
Normalize customer communication as a governed business capability rather than a collection of channel-specific sends.

## Boundary
`UnifiedCustomerCommunication` performs admission, suppression, validation and deterministic communication identity only. It does not call email, WhatsApp, social connectors, or providers.

Execution remains:
`BusinessAutomation -> Action Kernel -> durable execution/outbox -> governed connector/provider`.

## Guarantees
- workspace and customer identity are explicit
- opt-out/suppression blocks communication before execution
- email and WhatsApp share one communication identity model
- WhatsApp requires a selected governed account
- every admitted communication resolves to `customer_communication`
- no fuzzy AI capability mapping and no provider side effects
