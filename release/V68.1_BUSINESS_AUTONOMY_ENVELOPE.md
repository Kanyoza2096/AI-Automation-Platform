# V68.1 — Business Objective Contract & Bounded Autonomy Envelope

## Purpose

V68.1 starts Production Autonomy Certification by defining a whole-business
objective contract that can bound autonomous operation without becoming a
second authority plane.

## Contract

A `BusinessAutonomyEnvelope` declares:

- workspace scope
- business objective
- success metrics and evidence confidence
- explicit action allowlist
- risk boundary and approval triggers
- resource limits
- human escalation triggers
- operating mode: observe / suggest / execute
- validity window and lifecycle state
- optional lineage to an existing goal

## Authority boundary

The envelope has immutable `authority=none` and no execution, approval, or
policy effect. `execute` means the business has declared an operating mode;
it does **not** authorize a side effect.

Every proposed action must still pass the existing decision-policy,
approval, Action Kernel, durable outbox, execution ledger, and provider
execution/reconciliation boundaries.

The read-only evaluator in `services/business_autonomy_envelope.py` therefore
returns `requires_authoritative_recheck=true` even when an action is declared
eligible by the envelope.

## Relationship to existing goals

V68.1 does not replace `organism.goals.Goal` or the existing ConditionEngine
Goal. `goal_id` is optional lineage only. The envelope adds the missing
operating-boundary contract around a business objective rather than creating a
competing goal/execution engine.

## Certification status

- Contract implemented.
- Fail-closed validation implemented.
- Action declaration evaluator implemented as read-only.
- Persistence schema added in migration 061.
- Focused tests cover authority isolation, workspace/risk/action boundaries,
  expiry, escalation, and deterministic fingerprints.
- This phase does not claim production autonomy certification. V68.2+ must
  prove end-to-end integration across objective → decision → action →
  execution → outcome → learning → recovery → escalation.
