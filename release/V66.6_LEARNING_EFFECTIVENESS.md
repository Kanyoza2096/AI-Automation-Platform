# V66.6 — Learning Feedback + Effectiveness Evaluation

V66.6 closes the measurement loop around promoted learning. KDO can now evaluate
whether outcomes observed after a specific promoted knowledge item was used are
associated with better business results.

## Safety boundary

This is an observational evaluation, not causal proof. A positive lift does not
rewrite policy and does not authorize an action. `causal_claim` is permanently
`association_only` and `authority` is permanently `none`.

## Evidence requirements

- Feedback must explicitly identify `knowledge_id` and `outcome_observation_id`.
- Only explicit business success/failure contributes to the effectiveness sample.
- At least three determinate samples are required before effectiveness can be
  considered.
- A baseline cohort is required to call a result `effective`.
- Mixed positive/negative evidence is surfaced as `mixed`.
- Missing/uncertain evidence remains `insufficient_evidence` or `review_required`.

## What this enables

V66 now has a complete governed feedback chain:

Outcome observation → attribution → measurement → learning signal →
promotion → decision context → effectiveness feedback.

The result may inform future reasoning, but it cannot directly change policy,
approvals, Action-Kernel contracts, or execution authority.
