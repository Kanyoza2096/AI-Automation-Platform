# V68.11 — Business Event Identity & Condition Integrity

V68.11 hardens the real event-driven Business Automation path discovered while moving from workflow certification toward production operating journeys.

## Changes

- Event-driven automation now fails closed when `event_id` is missing.
- The stable event identity is preserved as the replay/idempotency fence.
- The Business Automation event bridge rejects unidentifiable events instead of converting them into a shared `missing` idempotency key.
- The `exists` condition operator now resolves bounded dotted paths consistently with `eq`, `truthy`, and the other condition operators.
- No new execution engine or authority path was introduced.

## Why this matters

Without a stable event identity, two distinct events could collapse onto the same generated idempotency key and suppress a legitimate business action. A dotted `exists` mismatch could also cause governed workflow conditions to behave differently depending on whether a field was nested.

These are operating-journey correctness issues, not cosmetic certification issues.
