# KDO V69.15 — Integration Policy & Approval Plane

V69.15 establishes the deterministic, tenant-scoped policy boundary between
integration capability and execution authority.

## Guarantees
- AI/LLM output cannot grant authority.
- Policy can only tighten platform risk floors; it cannot downgrade critical,
irreversible, or human-required actions.
- Tenant, connector, registry, capability, certification and execution state
are checked before an action can proceed.
- Approval is represented as a request/requirement, not an execution token.
- Forbidden/denied actions fail closed.
- Evidence is emitted for policy decisions when an evidence sink is supplied.
- No credentials are stored in the policy plane.
- No provider calls occur in this module.

## Approval ladder
`NONE < SUGGESTION < COUNCIL < HUMAN < FORBIDDEN`

The existing Council/DecisionGate/Action Kernel remain authoritative for their
respective approval and execution responsibilities. V69.15 does not bypass or
replace them.

## Validation
Focused V69.15 tests cover tenant isolation, capability readiness, explicit
denial, policy tightening, high-risk floors, and conservative defaults.
