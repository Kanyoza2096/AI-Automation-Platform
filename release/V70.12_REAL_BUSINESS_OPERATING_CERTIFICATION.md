# V70.12 — Real Business Operating Certification

## Purpose
Offline certification gate for the V70 Real Business Operating Layer.

## Proven controls
- explicit scenario/capability integrity
- catalog-only orchestration
- single governed operating facade
- cross-domain sequence execution
- tenant isolation
- duplicate and terminal replay fences
- deterministic certification evidence

## Not proven offline
- live provider credentials and external side effects
- production provider rate-limit behaviour
- production credential revocation behaviour
- persistence/restart behaviour against the live database

The certification status is therefore **NOT_PROVEN**, not falsely CERTIFIED.

## Architecture law
AI/planning/orchestration may interpret, prioritize and coordinate. Authority remains with the existing BusinessOperatingLayer, workflow admission, Action Kernel, durable outbox, governed connectors and reconciliation planes.
