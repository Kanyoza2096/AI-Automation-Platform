# V68.9 — Business Workflow Operating Certification

## Purpose

Certify the customer-facing workflow catalog as an operating contract, not merely
a list of available actions.

The certification checks that each workflow has a trigger, runtime action,
business outcome, operational requirements, valid risk classification, and an
explicit installation model. It also cross-checks the V68.8 capability execution
plane certification.

## Safety boundary

This phase is inspection-only. It never installs, enables, approves, executes, or
calls a provider. Certification metadata has no authority, execution effect,
approval effect, or policy effect.

## Important operating rule

Event-driven templates are translated at installation time into an explicit
`parameters.trigger_events` subscription. Scheduled/manual templates deliberately
do not use event subscriptions. New workflow templates remain disabled by default
until a workspace administrator explicitly enables them.

## Result semantics

- `CERTIFIED`: the catalog contract and execution-plane wiring are structurally sound.
- `REVIEW_REQUIRED`: the workflow is structurally sound but its existing governance
  boundary still requires human review, such as high-impact communication or deal actions.
- `BLOCKED`: a required customer-facing contract or execution-plane boundary is missing.

This is not live provider certification. Real CRM, notification, communication,
AI, and other third-party credentials remain deployment/runtime gates.
