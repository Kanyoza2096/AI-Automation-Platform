# V65.6 — Bounded Decision Candidate

## Objective
Bridge the V65.5 decision-input contract into the KDO decision plane without allowing
AI or the interpretation layer to manufacture authorization.

## Contract
`services/business_decision_candidate.py` converts a validated V65.5 decision input into
a deterministic, allow-listed candidate. The candidate contains a proposed action, risk,
approval requirement, governance state, resolution state and explicit constraints.

A candidate is **not** a decision. It has `authority: none`, and validation rejects any
attempt to change that. Only `decision_eligible` input can produce `status=proposed`;
`needs_review` becomes `review_required`, while `insufficient_evidence` becomes `blocked`.

## Safety properties
- No AI/provider details enter the candidate.
- Candidate action names are allow-listed.
- AI/deterministic disagreement remains visible.
- No connector is called and no external side effect occurs.
- Actual approval remains the responsibility of KDO's decision/policy layer and the
  Action Kernel.

## Next architectural step
V65.7 should wire these candidates into a formal decision-policy evaluation record,
without executing them, so every autonomous recommendation has an auditable reason,
risk classification and explicit approval disposition before V66 outcome learning.
