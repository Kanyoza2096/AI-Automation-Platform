# V66.1 — Outcome Observation + Attribution

V66 begins by separating **execution truth** from **business outcome truth**.

## Contract

`business_outcome_observations` records observations linked to an authoritative execution receipt. An execution receipt may establish that a provider accepted/completed an action, but it never by itself establishes business success.

The observation layer supports three types:

- `execution_receipt` — projected from the Action Kernel execution ledger.
- `business_outcome` — a later measurable business result attributed to an execution.
- `external_signal` — an external observation that may contribute evidence without becoming execution authority.

## Safety laws

- Observations are immutable facts/measurements, not commands.
- `authority` is always `none`.
- `unknown` outcomes cannot claim business success.
- Confidence is bounded to `[0,1]`.
- Idempotency is workspace + execution + observation type (+ external event id).
- Learning consumes observations; observations never modify authorization or past execution.

## Closed-loop direction

`Decision → Action → Execution Receipt → Outcome Observation → Attribution → Measurement → Learning`

V66.1 deliberately stops before adaptive policy mutation. Later V66 stages may derive learning signals, but those signals remain advisory and cannot bypass the V65 decision/policy/action boundaries.
