# V66.2 — Outcome Attribution + Measurement

V66.2 turns V66.1 observations into a conservative attribution layer.

## Contract

`business_outcome_attributions` links a measurable business outcome to a governed execution chain only when the evidence is explicit enough to identify one execution.

Attribution priority:

1. exact `execution_id` match;
2. explicit `external_event_id` mapping;
3. otherwise `unattributed`.

If more than one execution matches an external event, the result is `review_required`; KDO never guesses by timestamp, intent, or action name.

## Measurement

The measurement API keeps these concepts separate:

- execution success rate;
- business success rate;
- attributed conversion rate;
- average outcome confidence;
- unknown/uncertain observations;
- review-required attribution.

These are descriptive metrics. They do not alter policy thresholds, approval requirements, Action-Kernel contracts, or execution authority.

## Safety laws

- `authority` is always `none`.
- Ambiguous attribution fails closed into review.
- Unmatched outcomes remain visible rather than being force-attributed.
- Business success is never inferred from execution success.
- Attribution is workspace scoped and idempotent by `(workspace_id, observation_id)`.

## Closed loop

`Decision → Action → Execution Receipt → Outcome Observation → Attribution → Measurement → Learning`

V66.2 completes the attribution/measurement boundary but does not yet let learning mutate policy. That remains a later governed capability.
