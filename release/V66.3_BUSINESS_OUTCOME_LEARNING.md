# V66.3 — Business Outcome Learning

V66.3 converts measured and attributed business outcomes into bounded learning
signals. Learning is downstream of execution truth and outcome attribution.

## Safety boundary

Learning signals are evidence, not authority. They cannot approve actions,
change policy thresholds, alter action contracts, grant permissions, or bypass
DecisionGate, approval, the Action Kernel, durable outbox, or execution
reconciliation.

## Learning states

- `positive`: sufficiently confident attributed business success.
- `negative`: sufficiently confident attributed business failure.
- `uncertain`: business result exists but evidence is below the learning threshold.
- `insufficient_evidence`: attribution is not independently established.

## Key invariant

An outcome cannot become action-level learning merely because it happened near
an action in time or shared an intent. Attribution must be explicit.
