# V71.1 — Governed AI Workforce: Agent Identity

## Purpose
Establish a tenant-scoped identity plane for AI workers without conflating
identity with authority.

## Guarantees
- Deterministic identity fingerprint.
- Tenant isolation.
- Explicit lifecycle: proposed → active/suspended → retired.
- Duplicate and terminal transition fences.
- Integrity/tamper detection.
- Declared capabilities are descriptive only.

## Architecture law
AI identity does **not** grant permissions or execution authority. Tool
permissions, policy, risk, approvals, budgets, Action Kernel access and provider
execution remain separate governed planes.

## Not yet proven
V71.1 does not claim durable runtime persistence, production multi-process
concurrency, or live provider execution. Those require integration certification.
