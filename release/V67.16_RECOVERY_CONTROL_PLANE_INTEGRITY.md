# V67.16 — Recovery Control-Plane Integrity Certification

## Purpose

V67.15 made recovery closure truthful at the individual transition. V67.16
adds an independent integrity audit over the recovery plan, exception, and
Action Outbox projections so that contradictory state cannot silently present
itself as healthy.

## Invariants

- Recovery authority remains `none`.
- A recovered plan must be backed by the same workspace, intent, plan lineage,
  action type, and terminal outbox success.
- Released/recovered plans cannot be orphaned from their intent.
- Exceptions cannot claim `recovery_planned` or `recovered` without a linked plan.
- An exception marked recovered requires a linked recovered plan.
- AUTO_RECOVER plans require a positive bounded retry budget.
- Workspace and action lineage mismatches are critical integrity failures.
- The auditor is read-only and never repairs or executes anything.

## Safety boundary

The audit is diagnostic evidence only. It does not reset an outbox row, mark an
exception recovered, approve a retry, call a provider, or bypass the Action
Kernel/Council/DecisionGate.

## Certification

Focused V67.16 tests cover healthy lineage, false recovery, stale release state,
workspace mismatch, outbox plan mismatch, and exception/plan disagreement.
