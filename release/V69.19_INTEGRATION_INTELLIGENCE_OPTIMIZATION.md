# V69.19 — Integration Intelligence & Optimization

V69.19 adds a deterministic advisory optimization plane over observed integration metrics.

## Guarantees
- workspace-scoped evidence only
- reliability, ambiguity, latency, cost and business-outcome scoring
- bounded confidence based on sample count
- anomaly detection for reliability, latency and ambiguous execution
- deterministic ranking and tie-breaking
- insufficient evidence fails closed rather than guessing
- optimizer cannot grant authority or execute providers

## Authority boundary
Optimization produces recommendations only. Policy, approval, Action Kernel, runtime gateways and connectors remain the sole governed execution path.

## Validation
The V69.19 focused suite validates tenant isolation, ranking, evidence thresholds, anomaly detection, deterministic results, and the no-authority invariant.
