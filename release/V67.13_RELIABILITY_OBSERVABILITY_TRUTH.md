# V67.13 — Reliability Observability Truth Certification

## Objective
Ensure operational telemetry reflects authoritative execution and recovery truth.

## Guarantees
- `uncertain` execution is never counted as success or failure.
- uncertainty and open/planned recovery are visible as reconciliation debt.
- success rate is calculated only over known outcomes.
- invalid execution/recovery states fail closed.
- observability carries no execution authority.

## Scope
This is a deterministic certification contract and does not claim live provider or production monitoring certification.
