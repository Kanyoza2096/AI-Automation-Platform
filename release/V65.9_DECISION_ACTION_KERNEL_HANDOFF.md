# V65.9 — Decision → Action-Kernel Handoff Contract

V65.9 creates an explicit, durable admission contract between the policy decision plane and the Action Kernel.

## Safety law

A policy disposition is not execution authority. The handoff contains `authority=none`; the Action Kernel must independently validate action registration, risk, parameters, approval, workspace scope, idempotency, and durable acceptance.

## States

- `ready`: policy disposition `ALLOW`.
- `approval_required`: policy disposition `APPROVAL_REQUIRED`.
- `blocked`: `REVIEW` or `BLOCK`; no executable admission is represented.

## Event

`business.decision.action.handoff.ready`

The event is a durable signal for the governed automation plane. It does not call a connector and does not execute an external side effect.
