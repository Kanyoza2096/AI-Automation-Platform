# KDO V70.2 — Customer & Sales Operating Loop

## Purpose
V70.2 turns the Real Business Operating Layer into a stateful customer/sales loop.
It owns tenant-scoped business lifecycle state and deterministic routing, while the
existing BusinessOperatingLayer remains the sole execution facade.

## Lifecycle
`lead.created -> qualified -> follow_up_due -> engaged -> won/lost`
with `customer.opted_out` as a terminal suppression state.

## Safety boundary
V70.2 never calls providers, connectors, or Action Kernel directly. It delegates
through V70.1, preserving policy, approval, idempotency, outbox and reconciliation.

## Certification
- Cross-tenant events rejected.
- Duplicate event IDs rejected.
- Invalid lifecycle transitions rejected.
- Opt-out is terminal.
- Objective/workflow scope remains authoritative.
- State tables cannot represent authority or execution effects.

## Evidence debt
Live CRM/provider behavior remains dependent on the configured connector and its
V69 certification state. V70.2 does not claim provider certification by itself.
