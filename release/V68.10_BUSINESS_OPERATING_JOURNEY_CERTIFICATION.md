# V68.10 — Business Operating Journey Certification

## Purpose
V68.10 certifies representative end-to-end business operating journeys as compositions of the existing governed planes. It does not create a new execution engine and never installs, approves, or executes work.

## Certified journeys
- `sales.lead-to-followup` — event ingress → workflow admission → governed execution → business outcome.
- `sales.customer-signal-to-next-action` — customer signal → business understanding → decision governance → governed execution → business outcome. This remains `REVIEW_REQUIRED` at the consequential edge.
- `content.objective-to-publish-outcome` — objective → content intelligence → Guardian → governance → durable publish → external outcome → learning.

## Boundary
Certification is read-only. Authority, execution, approval, and policy effects remain `none`. No provider or connector is called by certification.
