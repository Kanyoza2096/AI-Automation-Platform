# V65.7 — Formal Decision-Policy Evaluation

V65.7 evaluates the V65.6 bounded decision candidate against a KDO-owned,
allow-listed decision policy and produces an auditable disposition:
`ALLOW`, `APPROVAL_REQUIRED`, `REVIEW`, or `BLOCK`.

The evaluator is deterministic, fail-closed, and side-effect-free. It does not
execute candidates, call connectors, or grant authority. Every evaluation has
`authority: none` and records policy version, risk, candidate state, governance
state, reason, and constraints.

## Boundary

`AI interpretation -> governance -> disagreement resolution -> decision input -> candidate -> policy evaluation -> approval/action kernel`

A policy disposition is not authorization. `ALLOW` means the candidate passed
this policy evaluation and may proceed to the existing governed approval/action
boundary; it does not itself execute the action.

## Verification

V65.7 focused tests cover allow, review, block, unresolved disagreement,
unknown action fail-closed behavior, and authority tampering.
