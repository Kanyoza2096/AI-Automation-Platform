# KDO V68.14 — Business Outcome Tenant Integrity

## Purpose

Harden the business-outcome plane so observations and attributions cannot cross workspace boundaries when linked to governed execution evidence.

## Changes

- Added `services/v68_14_business_outcome_tenant_integrity.py`.
- Business outcome attribution now filters candidates by the observation workspace before exact/external-event matching.
- Added explicit validation helpers for observation→execution identity and workspace equality.
- Added database triggers preventing cross-workspace outcome observations and attributions.
- No authority, approval, policy, or execution capability is introduced.
