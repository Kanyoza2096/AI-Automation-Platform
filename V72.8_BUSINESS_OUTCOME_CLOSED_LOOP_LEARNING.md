# V72.8 — Business Outcome Intelligence & Closed-Loop Learning

V72.8 aggregates V72.7 outcome-attribution evidence into durable-shaped, deterministic learning signals.

## Authority boundary
Learning is advisory evidence only. It cannot approve actions, mutate policy, grant authority, invoke providers, invoke Action Kernel, or rewrite the Business Constitution.

## Evidence semantics
- `LEARNED_PATTERN`: at least three consistently observed successful or unsuccessful outcomes.
- `MIXED_EVIDENCE`: some outcome evidence exists but is observational, associated, or mixed.
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`: insufficient measured outcome evidence.
- Association is never treated as causation.

## Canonical path
`Truth → Intelligence → Recommendation → Decision → Governed Execution → Result → Outcome → Attribution → Learning → Advisory Context`

Consequential commands remain `KCPG → Governance → Action Kernel → Durable Outbox → Connector/Provider → Reconciliation`.
