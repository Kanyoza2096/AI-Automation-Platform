# V73.8 — Trusted Business Context

## Purpose

V73 now separates three concerns:

1. **AI/business understanding** identifies what the customer appears to want.
2. **Decision policy** determines which bounded action is allowed.
3. **Trusted business context** supplies deterministic facts needed to materialize that action.

AI output and raw webhook/customer text are never used as scheduling authority.

## Follow-up example

`purchase_interest` maps to `crm_schedule_followup`. The ActionIntent materializer now asks `BusinessActionContextResolver` for trusted scheduling context.

The resolver accepts an explicitly trusted schedule or workspace policy. When no explicit schedule exists, it derives a bounded follow-up timestamp from the governed default of 24 hours. The value is generated by policy code, not by the model.

Bounds:

- minimum: 1 hour
- maximum: 720 hours
- invalid values fail closed to `needs_review`

The workspace policy can provide a deterministic `follow_up_hours` and trusted CRM contact identifier.

## Why this matters

This closes the gap between:

`customer intent → allowed action`

and:

`allowed action → complete executable parameters`.

It also avoids the unsafe alternatives of either inventing a date from model text or requiring every purchase-interest event to stop merely because the inbound message did not contain scheduling metadata.
